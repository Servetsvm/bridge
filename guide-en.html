1. Top bar and buttons

PartWhat it does
Board boxThe board number. Red edges mark the vulnerable side, D marks the dealer.
ContractThe contract and declarer, with tricks taken (“Us / Them”). Tap it to see the whole auction with explanations.
TodayToday's IMP total (or MP average) and the result of the last board.
New DealDeals a new board. In the middle of a board it asks once more (“Sure?”) so you cannot lose a board by accident.
UndoTakes back your last call or card, together with the robots' moves after it. Tap again to go further back.
HintShows what the robot would do in your place: the suggested call with its meaning, or the suggested card (it rises in your hand).

Settings / ResultsSettings, and the results/statistics screen.

Bid explanations: tap any call in the auction table to see what it means and which convention it belongs to; tap again to close. Calls with a dotted underline are conventional.

Last trick: during play, tap the green middle of the table to see the last finished trick (the winning card is outlined). Tap again, or wait 4 seconds, to go back.

End of the board: all four hands stay open on the table and the yellow banner shows the result and IMPs. Tap the banner for details (robot tables, normal contract, double-dummy result); Next deal starts a new board.

Partner's signals on defence: when your robot partner signals in the first tricks (a Lavinthal discard, or a high/low card on your lead), a yellow line under the table tells you what it asks for.

2. Practice a convention

Pick a convention from the menu (e.g. Cappelletti). From then on every new deal is chosen so that you (or your partner) get to use it. If it is switched off in your card it is switched on automatically.

  • Common conventions come up at once; rare ones (Smolen, Gerber…) can take 2-3 seconds to find.
  • If no suitable deal is found quickly, you get a normal deal and a short notice.
  • Choose Off — normal random deals to go back to normal play.

3. Play a specific deal

From a photo or screenshot

  1. Enter a deal → 📷 Read from a photo or screenshot → choose the picture.
  2. The four hands it read are shown. If a card is wrong, fix it in the code below.
  3. Play this deal.

It reads the end-of-board screen of your bridge app (four open hands drawn as card tiles) and hand records that list ♠ ♥ ♦ ♣ for each hand (ten written as “T” or “10”).

Privacy and memory: the picture is read on your device only. It is never saved, uploaded or kept by the app, and it is cleared from memory as soon as the cards are read. The cards that were read are forgotten when you start the deal or close the window. (The screenshot in your gallery is untouched — delete it yourself if you like.)

Typing the hands

Each hand as spades.hearts.diamonds.clubs, e.g. AKQ2.K73.J5.T942 (T = 10). Three hands are enough — leave the fourth empty and it is filled with the remaining cards. Or paste a PBN code: N:AKQ2.K73.J5.T942 … (four hands clockwise from North). Choose Dealer and Vulnerable.

4. Basic settings

SettingExplanation
Card styleClassic tiles: flat white card tiles side by side, like club bridge apps. Modern fan: overlapping fanned cards.
Your seatWhere you sit (North, East, South, West). Your hand is always at the bottom. When your side declares you play partner's hand too.
ScoringEvery board is also played at 10 expert robot tables. IMP: your score is compared with each table and converted to IMPs, then averaged (teams scoring). Matchpoints (%): the percentage of tables you beat (pairs scoring).
Opponents' systemSame as ours: the opponents use your card. Standard (SAYC): a plain standard system (Stayman, Jacoby 2NT, Gerber; no Lavinthal). Their calls are explained by their own system.
Robot speedHow fast the robots bid and play.
Bid explanationsWhether tapping a call shows its explanation.
Play a forced card automaticallyPlays your only legal card for you.
Delete score historyDeletes the score history on this device (cannot be undone).

Changes apply from the next deal. A deal is never dealt to you twice (except when you choose “Replay this deal”).

5. Our convention card

Base system: 5-card majors, 15-17 1NT, weak twos, strong 2♣, Jacoby transfers, negative and takeout doubles. Each convention below can be switched on or off; ✓ = on by default. Pairs marked “↔” replace each other: switching one on switches the other off.

ConventionShort description
✓ 2/1 Game ForceOver a major opening, an unpassed responder's new suit at the two level is game forcing (12+). 1NT is forcing for one round (6-12).
✓ RKCB 14304NT asks for five key cards: 5♣ = 1/4, 5♦ = 3/0, 5♥ = 2 without the trump queen, 5♠ = 2 with it.
✓ GerberDirectly over 1NT/2NT, 4♣ asks for aces: 4♦ 0/4, 4♥ 1, 4♠ 2, 4NT 3.
✓ Bergen RaisesOver 1M: 3♣ = 4-card support 7-9, 3♦ = 4-card 10-12, 3M = 4-card 0-6 (preemptive).
✓ Cappelletti ↔ DONTOver their 1NT: X penalty 15+, 2♣ one-suiter, 2♦ both majors, 2♥ = ♥ + a minor, 2♠ = ♠ + a minor, 2NT both minors.
✓ Jacoby 2NTOver a major opening: 4+ support, 13+, game forcing. Opener: new suit at 3 = singleton/void, at 4 = good 5-card side suit, 3M = 18+, 3NT = 15-17, 4M = minimum.
✓ Weak Jump Shifts ↔ Strong Jump ShiftsResponder's single jump in a new suit: 6+ cards, 0-5 HCP, to play.
✓ Stayman2♣ over 1NT (3♣ over 2NT) asks for a 4-card major.
✓ 4-Way TransfersOver 1NT: 2♦→♥, 2♥→♠, 2♠→♣, 2NT→♦.
✓ SmolenAfter 1NT–2♣–2♦, 5-4 in the majors with game values: bid the 4-card major at the three level, so the strong hand declares.
✓ Michaels / Unusual 2NTA cue bid of their suit = 5-5 two-suiter; a jump to 2NT = the two lowest unbid suits.
✓ SplintersDouble jump over a major opening = 4+ support, singleton or void in that suit, 12-15.
✓ Texas TransfersOver 1NT/2NT: 4♦→4♥, 4♥→4♠ (6+ major, game).
✓ Texas after interferenceTexas stays on when they double our 1NT or overcall at the 2-3 level. A bid in their suit is not a transfer: after a 2♥ overcall, 4♦ still shows hearts but a spade hand bids 4♠ directly.
✓ LebensohlAfter our 1NT is overcalled at the two level, 2NT forces opener to 3♣; a fast 3NT denies a stopper. A cue bid of their suit is Stayman: direct = no stopper, via 2NT = with a stopper.
✓ Lavinthal DiscardsDefensive signals: the suit discarded is not wanted; a high card asks for the higher of the two other suits, a low card for the lower (trumps and the suit led excluded). On partner's lead a high spot card encourages, the lowest discourages.
Inverted MinorsOver a minor opening: 2m = strong raise, 10+ (forcing); 3m = preemptive.
DruryA passed hand answers a third/fourth-seat 1M with 2♣ = 3+ support, 10-12.
New Minor ForcingAfter 1m–1M–1NT, the other minor is artificial and forcing, asking about a major fit.
Fourth Suit ForcingAfter three suits have been bid, the fourth suit is artificial and game forcing.
OgustOver partner's weak two, 2NT asks: 3♣ min/bad suit, 3♦ min/good, 3♥ max/bad, 3♠ max/good, 3NT AKQ.
Support DoubleAfter 1m–(P)–1M–(overcall), opener's double = exactly three-card support.
Responsive DoubleAfter partner's takeout double and their raise, double = 8+ with both unbid suits.
Strong Jump Shifts ↔ Weak Jump ShiftsResponder's jump in a new suit = 19+, slam interest.
DONT ↔ CappellettiOver their 1NT: X a one-suiter, 2♣ ♣ + a higher suit, 2♦ ♦ + a major, 2♥ both majors, 2♠ spades.
Gambling 3NTA 3NT opening = a solid 7+ card minor with no outside ace or king.

6. Sync between devices

Scores, statistics and the list of deals you have seen are kept in a private file on your GitHub account, so your phone and PC merge automatically. Offline, everything is kept on the device and merges when it is online again.

  1. Open github.com/settings/tokens/new?scopes=gist. Expiration: No expiration. Only gist ticked. Generate token.
  2. Copy the code starting with ghp_… (GitHub shows it only once).
  3. In the app: Settings → Sync → paste → Connect. Do the same on your other device.
The code works like a password — never share it. Disconnect removes it from the device.

7. Results and statistics

Statistics tab: boards played, total and per-board IMPs (or MP %), and total points for Today, This week, This month, This year and All time. Boards tab: date, contract, tricks, score and IMP/MP of the last 100 boards. Tap a board to see the four hands, the auction (tap a call for its meaning) and replay the play card by card at the table: ▶ plays the next card (◀ back, ⏮ ⏭ start/end, Play runs it by itself).

In the end-of-board details: the normal contract (the most common one at the robot tables), the double-dummy result of your contract and of the normal contract (tricks with perfect play), and each robot table's result. The screen shows both IMP and MP.

Play online with friends

  1. Everyone opens the app from the same link: https://servetsvm.github.io/bridge/. Nothing is dealt at first; Start plays alone with robots (also offline).
  2. When online, every player's table is listed in everyone's lobby: Open tables on the start screen and in the Online panel, each drawn as a small table with the player's name or “Robot” at every side. Refresh the list updates it.
  3. To join a table tap Ask to join, type your name, pick partner or opponent and tap Join. The request goes to the player at that table.
  4. When a request comes to you, tap Accept or Decline. The first time you accept, your table goes online and you are its host; the board you are playing carries on and the new player takes a robot's seat.
  5. The host can take a player off with Remove. The table owner can also open an empty waiting room with the host code (typed each time, never stored) and press Start when everyone is seated.
  • Your device runs the table (rules, robots, scoring): keep the app open while you play. Each player only receives the cards they are allowed to see.
  • Only the host deals a new board during play; after a board anyone can tap Next deal.
  • Undo takes back your own last call or card (and what was played after it); Claim and Replay work for everyone. The table chat tells everybody what happened.
  • Chat (top bar): write to the whole table or tap a quick message (Well played, Sorry partner, 👍…). The button shows how many messages you have not read.
  • Every player gets the board in their own results and statistics.
  • When a player's connection drops, a robot plays their seat at once; the seat is kept for them for 10 minutes and they get it back without asking when they return (the app reconnects by itself). A player who wants another seat asks with Sit here in the waiting room (or from the Online panel after a board); the host accepts or declines. Close the table ends the session; guests return to their own game.
  • The connection is direct between the devices. On some mobile networks it cannot be made — Wi-Fi usually works.

8. Installing on phone and PC

  • Phone: open the address in Chrome → ⋮ menu → Add to Home screen. It opens from its icon and works offline.
  • PC: the “Install” icon in the address bar of Chrome or Edge.
  • Updates: new versions install themselves the next time you open the app.
