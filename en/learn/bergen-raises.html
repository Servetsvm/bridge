Bergen Raises: 3♣ and 3♦ over 1♥ or 1♠, with Examples

Bergen raises show four-card support for partner’s major and your strength at once: 3♣ = 7–9, 3♦ = 10–12, three of the major = preemptive.

The idea

With four cards in partner’s major (a nine-card fit) it pays to tell partner at once. After 1♥ or 1♠, with four-card support:

  • 3♣ = 7–9 points (it says nothing about clubs).
  • 3♦ = 10–12 points, a limit raise.
  • 3 of the major = 0–6 points, preemptive.
  • With 13+ use Jacoby 2NT or a splinter.

With three-card support

A simple raise to two shows three-card support and 6–9 points. With three cards and 10–12 points bid a forcing 1NT first and then jump in partner’s major.

Opener’s next bid

Opener bids game with enough extra strength, or signs off in three of the major.

Example

Partner opens 1♥ and you hold ♠84 ♥KJ73 ♦A962 ♣1054 (8 points). Bid 3♣: four hearts, 7–9 points. With a minimum partner signs off in 3♥; with more partner bids 4♥.
