Michaels Cue Bid and the Unusual 2NT: Two-Suited Overcalls

The Michaels cue bid shows a 5-5 two-suiter over the opponents’ opening; the Unusual 2NT shows the two lowest suits. What partner bids, with an example.

The idea

When the opponents open at the one level, a direct cue bid of their suit shows two five-card suits:

  • Over 1♣ or 1♦: 2♣ / 2♦ = both majors.
  • Over 1♥: 2♥ = five spades and a five-card minor.
  • Over 1♠: 2♠ = five hearts and a five-card minor.

The Unusual 2NT

A jump to 2NT over their opening shows the two lowest unbid suits, usually both minors (5-5).

Partner’s answer

Partner picks the suit with the better fit at the cheapest level, or jumps to game with a good fit and 10+ points. After a Michaels bid over a major, 2NT asks which minor you hold.

Example

Right-hand opponent opens 1♥ and you hold ♠KQJ84 ♥3 ♦AQ1092 ♣54. Bid 2♥ (Michaels): spades and a minor. If partner bids 2NT, show your minor with 3♦.
