Jacoby Transfers over 1NT: Rules and Examples

Jacoby transfers after a 1NT opening: 2♦ shows hearts and 2♥ shows spades. Opener's reply, super-accept, responder's rebids and Texas transfers.

The idea

After partner's 1NT, a bid of 2♦ shows five or more hearts and asks opener to bid 2♥; 2♥ shows five or more spades and asks for 2♠. The strong hand becomes declarer, so the opening lead comes up to its high cards.

Any strength

The transfer is made with weak and strong hands alike. With a weak hand responder simply passes opener's reply.

Responder's next bid

  • Pass: weak.
  • 2NT: invitation with exactly five cards.
  • 3NT: game with exactly five; opener chooses between 3NT and four of the major.
  • Three of the major: invitation with six cards; four of the major: game with six.
  • A new suit: natural and forcing to game.

Super-accept

With four-card support and a maximum, opener may jump to three of the major (1NT–2♦–3♥) instead of the simple reply.

Example

Partner opens 1NT; you hold ♠KQ1063 ♥82 ♦J74 ♣953. Bid 2♥ and pass partner's 2♠: you play a part score in spades instead of a shaky 1NT.

Texas transfers

At the four level, 4♦ asks for 4♥ and 4♥ asks for 4♠: a quick way to reach game with a six-card major, again with the strong hand as declarer.
