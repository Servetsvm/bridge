Puppet Stayman over 2NT: How It Works, with Examples

Puppet Stayman: after a 2NT opening, 3♣ asks for a four- or five-card major. Opener’s answers, responder’s next bid and an example.

The idea

Over a 2NT opening (20–21, balanced) ordinary Stayman cannot find a five-card major in opener’s hand. With Puppet Stayman, 3♣ asks for both: a four-card or a five-card major.

Opener’s answers

  • 3♦: one or two four-card majors, no five-card major.
  • 3♥: five hearts.
  • 3♠: five spades.
  • 3NT: no four-card major.

After 3♦

Responder now bids the major they do not hold, so that the strong hand becomes declarer:

  • 3♥ = four spades; 3♠ = four hearts; 4♦ = four cards in both majors.
  • 3NT = no four-card major (responder was looking for a five-card major only).

Opener bids game in the major with a fit, otherwise 3NT.

Example

Partner opens 2NT and you hold ♠Q743 ♥K82 ♦J65 ♣Q94 (8 points). Bid 3♣. Over 3♦ bid 3♥ (four spades): partner bids 4♠ with four spades, otherwise 3NT. Over 3♥ (five hearts) bid 4♥: you have three of them.
