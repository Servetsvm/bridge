Roman Key Card Blackwood (RKCB 1430): Responses Explained

RKCB 1430 step by step: 4NT asks for the five key cards, the 5♣ 5♦ 5♥ 5♠ responses, asking for the trump queen and kings, and when to stop at five.

Key cards

Once trumps are agreed, 4NT asks partner how many key cards they hold: the four aces and the king of trumps, five in all.

The 1430 responses

  • 5♣: 1 or 4 key cards.
  • 5♦: 0 or 3 key cards.
  • 5♥: 2 key cards without the trump queen.
  • 5♠: 2 key cards with the trump queen.

In the 0314 version the first two answers are swapped: 5♣ shows 0 or 3, 5♦ 1 or 4.

The trump queen

After 5♣ or 5♦, the cheapest bid that is not the trump suit asks for the trump queen. Partner signs off in trumps without it, and with it bids a king or 5NT.

Kings

5NT promises that the side holds every key card and asks for kings: a grand slam is in sight.

When to stop

With two key cards missing, stop at five of the trump suit. With one missing, bid the small slam. With all five and enough tricks, look for the grand slam.

Example

Spades are agreed and you hold ♠AQJ95 ♥K4 ♦AKQ3 ♣Q2. Bid 4NT. Over 5♥ (two key cards, no queen) the two of you hold four of the five key cards, and you have the trump queen yourself: bid 6♠.
